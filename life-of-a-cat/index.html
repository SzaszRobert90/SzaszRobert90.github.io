🐱Who we are

Life of a Cat Studio is a small, independent animation studio. We make the Life of a Cat YouTube series: short, calm, made-for-kids episodes and songs starring Milo — sunbeams, cardboard boxes, farm friends, and songs the whole family can sing along with.

🎬What we make

  • Sing-along songs — original tunes written for little voices.
  • Little stories — cozy shorts about Milo's everyday adventures.
  • Guess-the-sound games — listen closely, who says moo?
  • Milo's Day — gentle full episodes that string the day together.

🛠️How it's made

Every episode is produced end-to-end by our own fully local animation pipeline — story, music, voices and pictures rendered on our own machines. The Life of a Cat Studio app name refers to this private, owner-operated production tool that publishes and manages our channel.
