Privacy Policy

🏠 Home 🔒 Privacy 📋 Terms

The short version

This website collects nothing. The Life of a Cat Studio app is a private, owner-operated production tool — it has no end users, no accounts, and no analytics or advertising trackers. Nothing you do on this site is recorded, and there is nothing here to sign up for.

About the Google authorization

Our production tool uses Google's OAuth to manage our own YouTube channel only. When the owner signs in, the app requests permission to upload videos and read that channel's own analytics and reporting data.

  • No one else can or needs to authorize this app.
  • Channel data fetched from YouTube (videos, statistics, analytics) is stored locally on the owner's own computer and is not shared, sold, or re-published.
  • We do not access data from any other YouTube channel or user.

Our use of YouTube API Services also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

Our videos are made for kids and are published with YouTube's "Made for Kids" setting. This website and the studio app, however, are aimed at the adult creator and rights holders — we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children, and there is nothing here to collect.

Contact

Questions or requests? Reach us through the Life of a Cat YouTube channel (About → business inquiry), or write to the address listed on that channel.
